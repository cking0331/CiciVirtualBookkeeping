About Cici

Cici Virtual Bookkeeping Services was built on a simple idea: small business owners shouldn't need an in-house finance team to have clean, reliable books.

We work remotely with sole traders and growing businesses across Australia, handling the reconciliation, reporting and compliance groundwork that BAS agents and accountants build on. Everything runs on documented, repeatable workflows — onboarding, reconciliation, BAS prep, payroll, month-end close and year-end handover — so your books stay consistent no matter how busy the month gets.
